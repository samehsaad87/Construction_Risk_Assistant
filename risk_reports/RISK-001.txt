Risk Analysis - RISK-001
Date Recorded: 2026-09-16

## Risk Description  
The concrete supplier may deliver materials two weeks late, causing potential project delays, disruption of work schedules, and increased costs.

## Probability  
Medium – While the supplier has generally been reliable, potential external factors (e.g., transportation issues, weather conditions) could cause delays.

## Impact  
High – Concrete is a critical material; a two-week delay could halt subsequent construction activities, extend the project timeline, and increase labor and overhead costs.

## Risk Level  
High

## Preventive Actions  
- Confirm delivery schedules and terms with the supplier before contract signing.  
- Maintain regular communication with the supplier to monitor order and shipping status.  
- Identify and prequalify backup concrete suppliers.  
- Schedule concrete delivery with buffer time ahead of critical milestones.  
- Monitor weather and transport conditions that may affect delivery.

## Response Plan  
- Activate backup supplier agreement to procure concrete if the primary supplier fails to deliver on time.  
- Adjust the project schedule to prioritize non-dependent tasks while awaiting concrete.  
- Communicate delays and revised timelines promptly with stakeholders.  
- Track additional costs incurred due to delay and document for claims or contract adjustments.  
- Evaluate the feasibility of partial deliveries or alternative materials if critical.

## Project Manager Recommendation  
Establish a firm contract clause with the primary concrete supplier that includes penalties for late delivery and incentives for on-time or early delivery to encourage adherence to the schedule.

Risk Analysis - RISK-003
Date Recorded: 2026-09-16

## Risk Description  
The concrete supplier may deliver materials two weeks late, potentially delaying the construction schedule, impacting dependent tasks, and increasing project costs.

## Probability  
Medium – There is a moderate chance that the supplier could face production or logistical issues causing a delay.

## Impact  
High – A two-week delay in concrete delivery can significantly affect project timelines, potentially leading to downtime for crews, extended equipment rental, and delayed subsequent construction phases.

## Risk Level  
High

## Preventive Actions  
- Confirm supplier capacity and delivery schedule before contract signing.  
- Establish clear delivery deadlines and penalties for late delivery in the contract.  
- Identify and pre-qualify alternative concrete suppliers.  
- Schedule regular communication and progress updates with the supplier.  
- Order critical concrete components earlier than usual to build buffer time.

## Response Plan  
- Expedite transportation or use express shipping options if delay occurs.  
- Re-sequence construction tasks to focus on activities not dependent on concrete delivery.  
- Mobilize additional labor or shifts once materials arrive to catch up on schedule.  
- Communicate delay to stakeholders early and adjust project timeline expectations as needed.  

## Project Manager Recommendation  
Establish a secondary concrete supplier as a backup before beginning the project, ensuring quick alternative sourcing if delays arise.

Risk Analysis - RISK-002
Date Recorded: 2026-09-16

## Risk Description  
Unexpected heavy rain may delay exterior construction activities by one week.

## Probability  
Medium – Weather patterns can be somewhat predicted, but sudden heavy rain is not uncommon during certain seasons.

## Impact  
Moderate – A one-week delay in exterior construction may affect project milestones and resource scheduling but may not completely halt the entire project.

## Risk Level  
Medium

## Preventive Actions  
- Monitor weather forecasts regularly, especially during critical exterior work phases.  
- Schedule exterior construction activities during seasons with historically lower rainfall.  
- Use temporary weather protection (e.g., tarps, tents) to shield ongoing work.  
- Plan for flexible resource allocation to accommodate potential weather delays.  

## Response Plan  
- Adjust the project schedule promptly to reflect delays caused by rain.  
- Reallocate resources to indoor or non-weather-dependent activities during rain delays.  
- Communicate delay impacts and updated timelines to stakeholders immediately.  
- Have contingency plans for expedited work once exterior conditions improve.  

## Project Manager Recommendation  
Establish a weather risk monitoring protocol and incorporate buffer time in the project schedule specifically for weather-related delays to minimize disruption.
